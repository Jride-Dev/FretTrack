Inventory and parts

Track parts and purchasing

Inventory helps your shop track parts, stock counts, vendors, purchase orders, receiving, purchase history, barcode labels, and job part usage.

Parts

A part can include name, part number, vendor, category, location, description, Vendor SKU, Vendor UPC, Barcode, manufacturer, unit cost, retail price, quantity on hand, reorder point, desired stock, special-order status, an optional image, and an optional purchase unit with the number of individual inventory units it contains.

Search and labels

Parts can be searched by name, part number, vendor SKU, barcode, category, or location. Barcode labels use stable FretTrack part identity such as FT-PART plus the part barcode code.

Vendors and purchase orders

  1. Create or select a vendor.
  2. Create a purchase order.
  3. Add existing parts or create new inventory parts from PO lines.
  4. Enter quantity, unit cost, shipping cost, and notes.
  5. Receive full or partial quantities when items arrive.

Receiving and landed cost

Receiving increases stock, records purchase history, and creates movement history. Purchase-order quantities use the saved purchase unit: receiving one 12-pack adds 12 individual inventory units. Partial receipts and historical receipts retain the conversion saved on their PO line even if the part setting changes later. When Add shipping to cost is enabled, inbound vendor shipping can be allocated into the received part cost.

Stock quantity safety

After a direct receive, stock adjustment, or purchase-order receipt, the open part editor refreshes its quantity and cost from the saved inventory result. Saving other part details afterward should preserve the new stock quantity.

Ordering for amplifier and keyboard jobs

Pro specialist repair benches can create a vendor purchase order linked to the current work order. Purchase quantity and package size describe what the vendor ships; job quantity describes what the customer repair needs. Receive the purchased package through Inventory first, then use Add to Parts & Payments on the specialist job to transfer only the saved job quantity into billing. Repeating a completed creation or fulfillment action reuses the existing linked record instead of creating another charge.

Permissions

Inventory write actions are controlled by shop role and shop access. Viewers can review inventory but should not create parts, receive purchase orders, adjust stock, or change vendor records.
