Estimates

Document repair estimates

Build the estimate from the work order, preserve exactly what the customer reviewed, and record approval or decline without rewriting history.

Estimate workflow

  1. Create the job and document the customer's requested work.
  2. Add intake photos and damage-map notes before quoting additional work.
  3. Add likely services and parts where the shop needs a cost or customer-facing line item.
  4. Save the work order, enter an audit note, and choose Mark Estimate Sent. FretTrack snapshots and locks the current totals.
  5. Record the customer's approval or decline with the communication method in the audit note.
  6. To change a sent or decided estimate, return it to draft, edit the scope, and send the next revision.

What to include

  • Requested work and customer concerns.
  • Condition notes that affect price or scope.
  • Parts that may be required.
  • Labor or service descriptions.
  • Approval method, such as phone, email, text, or in person.

Customer communication

Keep estimate notes clear and professional. Owners and admins control estimate state. Recording a decision does not send a message by itself, so use FretTrack email or the shop's approved contact method and describe that method in the audit note.

Parts and inventory

If an estimate depends on a part, use inventory or purchase order notes to show whether the part is in stock, needs to be ordered, or is customer supplied.

Locked totals protect the agreement

After an estimate is marked sent, its parts, services, discounts, and tax settings stay locked. Approval preserves that exact revision. If the scope changes, return it to draft and send a new revision rather than altering what the customer already reviewed.
