Billing and subscriptions

Understand plan access

FretTrack uses a 14-day Pro trial plus Shop and Pro subscriptions for repair-shop business use.

Plan basics

  • The standard trial provides 14 days of Pro access, requires no card, and does not automatically convert.
  • Shop is $29.99 monthly or $299.99 yearly and covers the paid core repair workflow.
  • Pro is $39.99 monthly or $399.99 yearly and adds the premium workflows below.
  • Pro adds Advanced Reporting, Team Members, Photo Editor, Amplifier Repair, Keyboard Repair, Scheduled Email, Automated Service Reminders, and Loyalty.
  • Expired access preserves shop data but may block writes and paid features.

Where to view status

Use the Billing or Shop Settings plan area to review current plan, billing interval, status, trial end, renewal or access end date, countdown, and locked premium features.

Who can manage billing

Billing and subscription controls are limited to shop owners and admins. Use Billing to open secure Stripe Checkout or the Stripe Billing Portal. Technicians and viewers cannot manage billing.

When access expires

Expired trial or subscription states should preserve customers, jobs, photos, inventory, and staff records. The app may allow safe viewing while blocking new writes until access is restored.

Annual savings

Shop yearly saves $59.89 and Pro yearly saves $79.89 compared with twelve monthly payments. Prices are USD. Applicable taxes, if any, are determined from billing details and shown at Checkout.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel anytime through the Billing Portal; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period. The first annual subscription purchase has a 14-day refund window. Monthly payments and renewals are non-refundable except for billing errors or when required by law.

Renewal and seller information

Annual subscriptions receive a renewal notice approximately 30 days before renewal. Keep the shop billing email current. FretTrack is operated by Jeffrey Russell d/b/a Torrance Guitar Repair and is offered for business use.

What the Pro automation features do

Scheduled Email handles a specific transactional job message up to 30 days ahead. Automated Service Reminders use separate customer consent and a shop-configured long-horizon follow-up rule after completed recurring service. Loyalty tracks qualifying paid/completed work-order stamps and explicit staff redemptions; it does not automatically discount an invoice or create store credit.

Need billing help?

Email support@frettrack-app.com with your shop name and account email.
