Current stable release

FretTrack 0.3.1

This maintenance release streamlines the application shell, Job Detail workflow, and stylesheet boundaries while preserving the complete intake-to-pickup behavior introduced in 0.3.0.

Open FretTrack Read the complete guide

Professional workspace and themes

The application now uses a grouped workspace navigation rail, a quieter control hierarchy, an operational Current Jobs summary, and consistent responsive behavior. Shops can select Use Device Theme or choose Bench Dark, Shop Light, Amber Tube, Seafoam, Blackguard, Burgundy Burst, Blue Steel, or High Contrast.

Screenshots show fictional shop and customer data.

Focused repair benches

Guitar, amplifier, and keyboard work orders open in focused repair benches while sharing one commercial workspace for parts, services, payments, messages, scheduling, photos, printing, and history.

Customer-ready documents

The Customer Service and Condition Report and invoice-style Job Sheet now use isolated print renderers, wait for required images, preserve billing totals, and use terminology appropriate to the instrument.

Professional communication

Immediate email, Pro Scheduled Email, opted-in Automated Service Reminders, immutable message snapshots, cancellation recovery, and Message History keep current customer communication attached to the work order. Provider-neutral conversation groundwork is being prepared for future inbound correspondence without presenting texting or two-way messaging as available today.

Inventory and purchasing

Parts, vendors, purchase orders, receiving, landed costs, barcode labels, specialist job purchasing, and explicit transfer into Parts & Payments support day-to-day shop work.

Shop operations

Scheduling, customer history, accounting-safe work-order exclusion, advanced reports, team assignment, loyalty, localization, usage limits, and permission-aware workflows are part of the stable product.

Paid access

New shops can register, confirm their email, and create a workspace without manual approval. The free-trial link opens account creation directly, and an interrupted post-creation profile load can safely retry the existing workspace. New shops receive a non-converting 14-day Pro trial. Shop is $29.99 monthly or $299.99 yearly; Pro is $39.99 monthly or $399.99 yearly. Stripe Checkout and the Billing Portal handle subscription enrollment and management.

What remains outside 0.3.1

Inbound email, two-way conversation views, SMS delivery, customer-report correspondence controls, public invoice links, full offline synchronization, customer instrument profiles independent of work orders, advanced accounting controls, dedicated AmpTrack and MidiTrack add-on modules that are planned later with more specialized amplifier and keyboard workflows, and deeper supplier or carrier integrations remain future work. FretTrack is repair-shop workflow software and does not replace professional accounting, tax, or legal advice.
