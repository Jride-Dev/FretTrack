# Customer Data And Privacy Note For Shops

FretTrack is repair-shop workflow and recordkeeping software. It helps shops manage customers, instruments, repair jobs, photos, work notes, parts, services, payments, communication history, and related records.

Access is shop-scoped and role-based. Users should see only the workspace and actions allowed by their shop membership, role, subscription state, and feature entitlements.

FretTrack is not intended for unnecessary sensitive personal information. Shops should enter only the information needed for repair workflow, customer communication, billing records, or legitimate business obligations.

Each shop remains responsible for the information it enters and for its own privacy notices, retention practices, legal requirements, and customer obligations. FretTrack does not sell shop workflow or customer repair data.

See the public [Privacy Policy](https://frettrack-app.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://frettrack-app.com/terms).
