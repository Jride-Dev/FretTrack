# Customer Data & Privacy Note for Beta Shops

FretTrack is repair-shop workflow and recordkeeping software. It helps repair shops manage customers, instruments, repair jobs, job photos, work notes, parts, services, and related shop records.

Shops using FretTrack enter customer, instrument, job, photo, and repair data so they can document intake, track work, communicate clearly, and keep repair history organized.

Access in FretTrack is shop-scoped and role-based. Users should only see the shop workspace and features their account and role are allowed to access.

FretTrack is not intended for unnecessary sensitive personal information. Shops should avoid entering data that is not needed for repair workflow, customer communication, billing records, or legal/shop obligations.

Each shop remains responsible for what it enters into FretTrack and for meeting its own local legal, privacy, customer-notice, retention, and business obligations.

FretTrack does not sell shop workflow or customer repair data.

For the current public legal pages, see the FretTrack Privacy Policy at `https://frettrack-app.com/privacy` and Terms of Service at `https://frettrack-app.com/terms`.
